AntennaAlign

Aim directional antennas with augmented reality, maps and terrain profiles.

Support Privacy policy

The source code is not public.

Features

  • Pick a target on the map

    Tap a topographic or satellite map, or type coordinates. Your own site comes from GPS, or you can place it by hand.

  • Bearing, distance and tilt

    True bearing, distance and the exact tilt for your antenna heights, with earth curvature and atmospheric refraction included.

  • Terrain profile

    See the ground along the whole path, with line of sight and first Fresnel zone clearance for your frequency.

  • Augmented-reality alignment

    Point the phone along the antenna and follow the turn and tilt guidance on the camera view until it says on target.

  • Terrain overlay

    A wireframe of the surrounding hills is drawn over the camera, and the app warns you when a ridge blocks the target.

  • Compass you can trust

    Magnetic declination is corrected automatically. Sync heading on a landmark you can see, or set compass and tilt offsets.

  • Saved targets

    Keep the masts and repeaters you work with, along with their frequency and antenna height.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, ads or analytics. Your location, camera view and saved targets stay on your phone.
